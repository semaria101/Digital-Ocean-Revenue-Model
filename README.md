# DigitalOcean Revenue Model (2026 Forecast)

This repository contains a financial model analyzing DigitalOcean’s revenue performance using ARR, ARPU, and Net Dollar Retention (NDR) metrics.  
The model evaluates historical trends, calculates key SaaS KPIs, and provides a structured revenue forecast through 2026.

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding DigitalOcean’s monetization and customer expansion efficiency by analyzing:

- **ARR (Annual Run-Rate):** Growth trend from $794M (2024) to $919M (2025)
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** Increasing customer spend from **$100.71 (2024)** to $116.20 (2025)
- **NDR (Net Dollar Retention):** Improvement from 97% to 99% in the latest period
- **Implied Customer Volume:** Identifying the divergence between revenue growth and subscriber contraction
- **Forecasting:** Applying trends to project future revenue performance through 2026

The goal of this model is to highlight how DigitalOcean is expanding revenue through higher customer monetization, improving retention efficiency, and reducing reliance on net-new users to drive topline growth.

## Key Insights

- **ARR grew 15.7% YoY**, reflecting strong recurring revenue momentum.  
- **ARPU increased from $100.71 → $116.20**, demonstrating deepening monetization of existing customers.  
- **NDR improved from 97% → 99%**, signaling healthier expansion from the current customer base.  
- The model indicates that revenue growth increasingly depends on **higher spend per customer**, offsetting flat-to-declining customer counts.
